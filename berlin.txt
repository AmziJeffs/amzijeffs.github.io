Bodhicitta Vegan Bowl (https://maps.app.goo.gl/YdUnTYChJP9mz4sS9) -- Extremely good pho. When I visited in 2022, the son of the owner happened to be my waiter and he talked really proudly about their process for making the broth and everything. Very special.

Sahara Imbiss (various locations) -- East African food, more casual but super tasty. I always get the tofu vegan teller. Whatever you get make sure it has erdnusssauce (peanut sauce).

Chapter One Coffee (https://maps.app.goo.gl/2J7t3TAxnCTa62eh7) -- Extremely third wave coffee, best I've ever had. Worth a visit just to see their crazy filter coffee machine (https://maps.app.goo.gl/ddLVxyWWUpbDB1zKA).

Feel Seoul Good (https://maps.app.goo.gl/6uRK3jEvxL9yQP8BA) -- Great Korean food. The inside has traditional-style Korean seating (very low to the ground) which is cool, and the food is on point.

Secret Garden (https://maps.app.goo.gl/j598X2QAfLyz3gvd9) -- Vegan sushi, kind of neat second floor location. And the neighborhood around there is cool generally, there are big flea markets along the river just East of it on weekends.

Misc: Make sure to get a Club Mate at one of the corner stores. It's a sort of carbonated tea beverage, amazing on a hot day.
